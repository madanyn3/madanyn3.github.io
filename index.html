Madan Y N

Silicon Design Engineer II, Server CPU Performance Modeling
AMD, Bengaluru, India

{% include social-links.html %}

I build models of computer systems, and I try to work out how far those models can be trusted. At AMD I model new cache-hierarchy features in a cycle-accurate CPU simulator, run bottleneck analysis and design-space exploration before the architecture is finalized, and check the models against RTL.

I graduated from IIT Bombay in 2024 with a B.Tech (Honours) in Electrical Engineering and a minor in Computer Science and Engineering. There I built analytical latency models for transformer inference on FPGAs at TCS Research, which won the Best Poster Award at the HiPC 2023 Student Research Symposium. For my bachelor's thesis, with Sunil Shenoy (VP Emeritus, Intel) and Prof. Laxmeesha Somappa, I studied the role of the ISA in CPU performance using gem5.

Lately I have been modeling a system nobody designed: a self-directed study, built on a closed-loop cardiovascular model, of whether a hypothetical auxiliary pump could keep vital organs perfused after the heart stops. It showed me where my judgment runs out: choosing and trusting stiff ODE solvers, deciding which parameters a model can identify, and quantifying uncertainty. That is the mathematics I want to study next.

Interests

  • Modeling and simulation of complex systems
  • Numerical methods for ODEs and PDEs
  • Verification and uncertainty quantification
  • Computer architecture and performance modeling
